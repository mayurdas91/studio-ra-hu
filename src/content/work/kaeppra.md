## The context

Kaeppra is a retail business serving customers in Delhi and the NCR. It was starting out without a brand of its own, and it ran its stock and sales without a clear, shared view of the numbers.

## The challenge

The business needed to be recognised and remembered, and it needed to know what was happening inside the shop. Without a name and look that stood out, it blended in with other retailers. Without inventory tracking, the owners could not easily see which products were selling, which were sitting idle, and where their capital was tied up.

## What we did

We started with the brand. We chose the name Kaeppra so that it sounds regal and is easy to recall, then designed the logo and set the brand colours around it. We carried that identity into visiting cards, shop banners and signboards, so a customer sees the same brand on the street, in the shop and in their hand.

Then we turned to operations. We set up an inventory system in Excel and built a dashboard on top of it that reports on stock and gives the owners a business-intelligence view of the shop. The dashboard flags the products on which the business is losing capital, so slow movers are spotted early instead of discovered late.

We also helped Kaeppra become part of a new community of Delhi and NCR retailers. Being connected to other retailers brought referrals, shared know-how and new business opportunities.

Keeping track of inventory brought practical advantages to the shop:

- A clear view of what is in stock and what is running low, so reordering is planned rather than rushed.
- Less capital sitting in slow-moving products.
- Fewer missed sales caused by stockouts.
- Faster, simpler stock counts and sales reconciliation.
- Decisions based on the shop's own numbers instead of memory and guesswork.

The same systems cut the time spent on routine operations: counting and checking stock, deciding what to reorder, reconciling sales and preparing reports. Across these tasks the time saved averaged about 6 hours.

## The outcome

Kaeppra now has a regal, memorable name and a consistent visual identity across its cards, banners and signboards. It runs on an inventory system that shows the owners where their money is working and where it is stuck.

The work helped Kaeppra gain 11 more stable leads and increased its monthly earnings by an average of ₹2 lakh. It also saved an average of about 6 hours across operations, which the team could put back into running and growing the business.
