## The context

The Custom Craft (thecustomcraft.in) is a business built around 3D printing. Its customers were still learning what 3D printing can do, and its website needed to explain the craft as well as sell it.

## The challenge

A specialised business wins trust by showing that it knows its subject. The website needed a layout that guided visitors, content that answered their real questions, and a body of writing that told search engines and customers alike that The Custom Craft is a credible voice on 3D printing.

## What we did

We began by researching 3D printing itself, so that everything written for the business rested on real understanding of the subject and not on generic copy.

From that research we worked out the layout of the website and the content for each part of it, so that visitors could find what they needed and move towards an enquiry or an order.

We then researched the topics the business should write about to improve its topical authority. We grouped them into themes that suit the questions customers ask, for example:

- How 3D printing works and what it can make.
- Materials and how to choose between them.
- Designing a part so that it prints well.
- Finishing and care for printed objects.
- Ideas for custom and personalised products.

Finally, we wrote the blogs for those topics. Each one is written for a reader who is curious or ready to buy, links naturally to the related pages of the site, and builds the business's standing on the subject one article at a time.

## The outcome

The website now has a clearer layout, content shaped around customer questions, and a growing library of blogs that supports its standing on 3D printing.

Footfall on the website increased by 46%, and the share of visitors converting into customers improved by 22%.
